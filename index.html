Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, and scientific machine learning. In recent work, I have studied heat transfer and mixed convection in arc-shaped, triangular, prismatic, and lid-driven cavity geometries, including nanofluid systems with thermal radiation and Joule heating [1] [2] [3] [4]. I also explore nonlinear evolution equations for viscoelastic fluids and use artificial neural networks, including Levenberg-Marquardt backpropagation, to build predictive models from simulated data [1] [2]. My goal is to develop reliable, deployable methods that connect theoretical physics with practical engineering applications.
